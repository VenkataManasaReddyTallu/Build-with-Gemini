# My agent: sous-chef-agent
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks plan meals, customize recipes, and manage ingredients with a catalog of recipes and grocery items.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Dietary preferences, pantry inventory, cook time constraints, household size.
- Tools: Ingredient substitution calculator, seasonal ingredient lookup, step-by-step cooking timer manager.
- Catalog/UI: Recipe cards, ingredient lists, weekly meal plan table.
- Image gen: AI-generated food dish presentation photos.
- Sandbox: Scaling recipe yield, nutritional macro calculations, and cooking time adjustments.

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): A2UI recipe cards, image gen dish preview, nutritional macro calculator in sandbox
First eval question: "What can I cook with chicken thighs, cilantro, and limes that is gluten-free and under 30 minutes?"
